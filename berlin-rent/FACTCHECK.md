# Fact-check: "Why can't anyone in Berlin find a flat?"

Independent check, 8 Oct 2026. Files checked: `index.html`, `data.js`, user-visible strings in `app.js` / `map.js`. The `research/` folder, `data-map.js` and `geo.js` were not opened. Claims that only `data-map.js` could confirm are marked UNVERIFIED (data-derived).

Verdicts: **WRONG** = factually incorrect or contradicted on the page. **NEEDS NUANCE** = true in substance but misleading as worded. **UNVERIFIED** = no primary source found. **OK**.

## 5. Five claims I am least confident about

1. **Population growth "about 375,000"** (N1). The raw endpoints are right, but BBU's own census-adjusted growth figures are inconsistent with each other (+360k for 2011–2024, then +422k for 2011–2025 despite only +15k in 2025). The cleanest consistent-basis number is unclear.
2. **"In Berlin, a booked Bürgeramt appointment counts as registering on time."** Confirmed only via TU Berlin's international office and a third-party guide, not in an official berlin.de text I could open.
3. **DSK: "job tenure off-limits."** The DSK text on "Dauer der Beschäftigung" was truncated in my fetch. Nationality and tenants'-union are clearly inadmissible; tenure is very likely but not fully read.
4. **GSW debt "€1.56bn".** BBU/taz/kaz say 1.56 (or "over 1.5"), Welt says ~1.7bn. The BBU press text cut off before the debt figure.
5. **Morgenpost 15 Apr 2026 spokesman quote (160,000 vs 60,000)** and the **1998 8.5% vacancy (155,000)**. Neither primary source was found, and IBB's 2000-era report suggests ~6% vacancy.
