## 2. The document gauntlet

- **SCHUFA-BonitätsCheck (the shareable version): €29.95**, as a PDF or by post. Source: SCHUFA AGB, "Stand Juni 2026", https://www.meineschufa.de/check/AGB_BC_Stand_Juni_2026.pdf. The same price is shown by Stadtsparkasse Düsseldorf, https://www.sskduesseldorf.de/de/home/service/schufa-bonitaetscheck.html (undated page).
- **Free SCHUFA Datenkopie (Art. 15 GDPR):** free, but it carries **no verification code** and contains every account and loan. Banks advise **not** to give it to landlords. Source: Deutsche Bank, https://www.deutsche-bank.de/pk/service-und-kontakt/kooperationen/schufa-bonitaetscheck.html (undated page; says "einmal im Jahr"). Note that Art. 15(3) GDPR makes the first copy free; further copies may cost a reasonable fee.
- **Mietschuldenfreiheitsbescheinigung (rent-debt-free certificate):** a letter from the previous landlord saying you owe nothing.
  - **BGH, 30.09.2009, VIII ZR 238/08:** the old landlord is **not obliged** to issue one. They only owe receipts (Quittungen, §368 BGB). Source: https://www.bundesgerichtshof.de/SharedDocs/Pressemitteilungen/DE/2009/2009199.html. Verified.
  - **New (Jan 2026):** the DSK Orientierungshilfe V2.0, issued jointly by all German data-protection authorities and hosted by the Berlin commissioner, concludes that a new landlord therefore **cannot demand** one. https://www.datenschutz-berlin.de/fileadmin/user_upload/pdf/publikationen/DSK/orientierungshilfen/2026-DSK-OH-Mietinteresse-V2.pdf (Stand Januar 2026).
- **What a landlord may ask, and when** (same DSK V2.0, Jan 2026, plus its model questionnaire https://datenschutzkonferenz-online.de/media/oh/OH_Einholung_von_Selbstauskuenften_Mietinteressenten_V2_Anhang.pdf):
  - **(A) At the viewing:** name and address only. They may *look at* your ID and note that they checked it. **A photocopy of your ID is "not necessary and therefore not permitted."** WBS flats: they may ask whether you have a WBS, but may not copy it yet.
  - **(B) Once you say you want the flat:** number of people moving in, employer and occupation, net income. A threshold yes/no ("do you have at least €X") is enough. Also: pets (not small caged ones), open insolvency, eviction title in the last 5 years.
  - **(C) Only after they pick you:** proof of income (payslip, bank statement or tax notice, **with irrelevant data redacted**) and a credit check. If you provide a bonity certificate, they may not run their own credit query. Rent-arrears yes/no questions are allowed.
  - **Not allowed at all:** nationality, religion or ethnicity; criminal record or ongoing investigations; marriage plans, pregnancy or wish for children; membership in a party or a **tenants' association**; **how long you've been employed**; the reasons for your monthly debts; **contact details of your previous landlord**; "consent" clauses in the form (consent is never "free" here).
- **"Last 3 payslips"** is a market norm, not a legal requirement. DSK only allows proof of current income at stage C, with redactions. I found no rule that says "3".
- **WBS:** required by law (§27 WoFG) before moving into a socially funded flat. Same DSK document.

## 3. The Anmeldung chicken-and-egg

- **§17(1) BMG:** register **within two weeks** of moving in. https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bmg/__17.html
- **§19 BMG:** the landlord (Wohnungsgeber) **must** confirm the move-in in writing within that deadline. Only the landlord or their agent may issue it. **§19(6):** offering an address for registration to someone who will not actually live there is **prohibited**. https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bmg/__19.html
- **§54 BMG fines:** a fake address (§19(6)) → **up to €50,000**. Late or no registration, or a landlord not confirming or issuing a false confirmation → **up to €1,000**. https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bmg/__54.html. **Verified.**
- **Berlin twist:** a *booked* appointment counts as meeting the deadline, so "you commit no offence even if your appointment is after the deadline". service.berlin.de/terminvereinbarung, https://service.berlin.de/terminvereinbarung/ (undated page).
- **Online Anmeldung** has been available in Berlin since **October 2024** (The Berliner, 2024-10-16, https://www.the-berliner.com/english-news-berlin/online-anmeldung-berlin-allows-digital-registration/). ⚠️ But per service.berlin.de it is **only for German or EU citizens moving *within* Germany** who hold an eID. It does not work with a non-EU residence card (eAT). https://service.berlin.de/dienstleistung/120686/ (undated page). **So the newly arrived non-EU expat, the classic expat case, still needs an in-person appointment.**
- **Berlin registrations:** about **500,000 per year**, nearly a quarter of all Bürgeramt appointments. Pankower Allgemeine Zeitung, https://www.pankower-allgemeine-zeitung.de/wohnsitzanmeldung-im-land-berlin-digital/ · 2024-10-20
- **Waiting times:**
  - Q2 2024: an average of **33 days** (34 a year earlier). Marzahn-Hellersdorf: **41 days**. Wegner's promised 14-day target had not been met. **45,668 no-shows** in Apr–Jun 2024 (69,703 a year earlier). Tagesspiegel, https://www.tagesspiegel.de/berlin/14-tage-ziel-auch-unter-schwarz-rot-in-weiter-ferne-in-diesen-berliner-bezirken-dauert-das-warten-auf-einen-burgeramtstermin-jetzt-sogar-noch-langer-12517826.html · 2024-10-11
  - Sept 2025: **average wait 27 days**. The 14-day target is met in **4 of 5 cases** (60% in 2024). 180,000 appointments a month. Morgenpost, https://www.morgenpost.de/berlin/article409956334/wie-es-mit-der-grossen-verwaltungsreform-nun-weitergeht.html · 2025-09-09
  - Jan 2025: a search on 29 January found the first appointment on **26 March**. By Dec 2025, same-day slots were available. Berliner Kurier, https://www.berliner-kurier.de/berlin/von-chaos-zu-ordnung-berliner-buergeraemter-ueberraschen-mit-freien-terminen-li.10010253 · 2025-12-15
- **Tax ID:** the Bürgeramt passes your data to the BZSt, which **mails** your Steuer-ID to your registered address. If nothing arrives within 3 months, you can ask. service.berlin.de, https://service.berlin.de/dienstleistung/329123/ (undated page). BZSt plain-language page: "may take 4 weeks", https://www.bzst.de/DE/Service/LeichteSprache/steuerid/steuerid.html. **BZSt FAQ: "Für eine erfolgreiche Zustellung muss der Nachname am Briefkasten angebracht sein"** (your surname must be on the letterbox), https://www.bzst.de/DE/Privatpersonen/SteuerlicheIdentifikationsnummer/FAQ/faq_node.html (undated page).
- **The loop:** flat → landlord's confirmation → Anmeldung → tax ID (by post, to a letterbox with your name on it) → payroll and bank → payslips and SCHUFA → flat.
  - The legal parts are sourced above.
  - "Banks require Anmeldung" is common practice, but I did **not** verify a statute. A basic account (Basiskonto, Zahlungskontengesetz) is a legal right; I did not check whether it needs an address. **Treat the bank link as "commonly", not "always".**

Berliner Mieterverein magazine, https://www.berliner-mieterverein.de/magazin/online/mm1225/berlin-verliert-massenhaft-bezahlbaren-wohnraum-durch-moeblierte-vermietung-auf-zeit.htm · 2025-12-03. taz, 2025-10-29: the IFSS author estimates **about 40%** of listings are furnished or temporary.
- ⚠️ **Conflicting source:** vdp (Pfandbriefbanken) puts Berlin's furnished share at a peak of **"knapp 25%"** (2023), with surcharges up to 100% in 2022. https://www.pfandbrief.de/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/vdpR-Marktaspekte_Moebliertes-Wohnen_2025_06_V3.pdf · June 2025. The dataset and definition differ (all furnished vs. MWZ incl. temporary). **Use the IBB/Senate series and cite the range honestly.**

**Zweckentfremdungsverbot (ban on misusing housing, e.g. as holiday rentals):**
- In force in Berlin since 2014-05-01.
- **Fines up to €500,000** for unauthorised misuse (§7(1) no. 1–4 ZwVbG). **Up to €250,000** for missing or false registration numbers etc. (§7(1) no. 5–11). Verified: https://www.berlin.de/sen/stadt/_assets/service/formular-center/bereich-wohnen/bauwohn600_25.pdf and the ZwVbG text https://www.lexaris.de/book/version/documentflat/head/1252593
- **Standard fine** (the realistic number): **€1,500–2,500 per flat per month** for an unauthorised holiday rental; **€1,000** for a first missing registration number. Senate "Mitteilung 1/2026", https://www.berlin.de/sen/wohnen/_assets/rechtliches/durchfuehrung-des-zweckentfremdungsverbot-gesetzes-mitteilung-1-2026_neufassung-der-bussgeld-rahmenbetraege.pdf (2026)
- **Second homes:** may be rented as holiday flats on at most **90 days/year**, with a permit.
- **EU Regulation 2024/1028** applies from 2026-05-20, and Berlin's implementing law has been in force since 2026-06-17. But **new registration numbers are not being issued yet** because the technical system is still being tested. https://www.berlin.de/sen/wohnen/rechtliches/zweckentfremdungsverbot/ (as of 2026-10-08)

## 8. Expat misconceptions → the actual rule

1. **"Rent is rent."** No. *Kaltmiete* (net cold) + *Nebenkosten/Betriebskosten* + heating = *Warmmiete*. The **deposit cap and the Mietpreisbremse are calculated on net cold**: §551 BGB says "ohne … Betriebskosten"; §556d refers to the ortsübliche Vergleichsmiete, which is net cold per the Mietspiegel table (https://mietspiegel.berlin.de/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/mietspiegeltabelle2026.pdf).
2. **"The deposit is whatever the landlord says."** Max **3× net cold**, payable in **3 instalments**, kept separately (§551 BGB).
3. **"You always pay the Makler."** Not since 2015-06-01: whoever *orders* the agent pays (§2(1a) WoVermittG). Advances are banned (§2(4)).
4. **"Furnished flats are exempt from the rent brake" / "furnished flats are illegal."** Both wrong. Furnished letting is legal. The **Mietpreisbremse still applies** unless use is *genuinely* temporary (§549(2) BGB). The surcharge is capped at about 2% of the furniture's current value per month (Senate guideline 2024). Holiday letting (<long-term) needs a permit under the ZwVbG.
5. **"You can't be evicted in Germany."** False. Landlords can terminate for e.g. **own use (Eigenbedarf)** or serious breaches (§573 BGB). Their notice period is **3 months, rising to 6 after 5 years and 9 after 8 years** (§573c(1), https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__573c.html). Tenants don't need a reason.
6. **"I can leave with a month's notice."** Tenant notice is **3 months** (given by the 3rd working day of a month, effective at the end of the month after next, §573c(1)). It is longer if you agreed to a Staffelmiete waiver (max 4 years, §557a(3)).
7. **"I must repaint everything when I leave because the contract says so."** Often **void**: a standard-form renovation clause is invalid if the flat was handed over **unrenovated** without fair compensation. Clauses with **rigid fixed intervals** are also invalid (BGH 18.03.2015, VIII ZR 185/14, VIII ZR 21/13; https://www.bundesgerichtshof.de/SharedDocs/Pressemitteilungen/DE/2015/2015039.html). In VIII ZR 185/14, a discount of **half a month's rent was not enough** compensation.
8. **"The landlord can ask anything and copy my passport."** No. See §2 above: an **ID copy at the viewing is not permitted**; questions about nationality and job tenure are not allowed (DSK 2026).
9. **"Rent cap = the price can't be over the Mietspiegel."** It's **+10%** over the local comparative rent. **Exceptions:** first lettings of flats first used after **2014-10-01**, the first letting after comprehensive modernisation, and a higher previous tenant's rent (§§556d–f). The landlord **must disclose the exception before you sign**, or they can't rely on it (§556g(1a)). In Berlin the Mietpreisbremse applies until **2029-12-31** (Senate ordinance in force 2026-01-01, https://www.parlament-berlin.de/ados/19/IIIPlen/vorgang/verordnungen/vo19-298.pdf; federal extension passed by the Bundestag on 2025-06-26, https://www.bundestag.de/dokumente/textarchiv/2025/kw26-de-mietpreisbremse-1084786).
10. **"If I complain after signing, I'm out of luck."** You can sign first and complain later. You must send a written complaint (*Rüge*, in text form). A complaint **within 30 months** of the lease starting lets you reclaim overpayments from the start; after that, only from the complaint onward (§556g(2) BGB, version in force since 2020-04-01 for contracts concluded from then on). Pitfall: older advice (bmgev) still says money is recoverable only from the complaint onward; that applies to pre-April-2020 contracts.
11. **"Anmeldung is optional / only for Germans."** Mandatory for everyone within **2 weeks** (§17 BMG). Fine up to €1,000. Online registration is not available to non-EU arrivals (service.berlin.de).
12. **"A Mietschuldenfreiheitsbescheinigung is mandatory."** No legal duty to issue one (BGH 2009). Data-protection authorities say landlords **can't demand** one (DSK 2026).

## 11. Flags on the brief's assumptions

- ✅ **Deutschlandticket €63 from Jan 2026:** correct. Add: **€66.80 from 2027-01-01**, announced 2026-09-30.
- ⚠️ **"~40–50% furnished":** the IBB/Senate series gives **48% in 2025** (peak **57% in 2023**). The IFSS estimate is ~40%. **But vdp says only ~25% at peak.** Definitions differ; cite the IBB series and note that listings ≠ flats.
- ✅ **Mietschuldenfreiheitsbescheinigung:** BGH 30.09.2009 VIII ZR 238/08 confirmed. Stronger, as of 2026: the DSK says landlords **cannot demand** it.
- ✅ **Fines:** late Anmeldung up to €1,000 and Scheinanmeldung up to €50,000 are both correct. Nuance: the €50k offence is *offering or providing an address* (§19(6)), which covers "selling" a confirmation. A **non-landlord issuing** a confirmation is the €1,000 offence (§54(2) no. 4).
- ⚠️ **"Need Anmeldung for a bank account":** common practice, but **not verified as a legal requirement**; the Basiskonto right exists. Phrase it softly.
- ⚠️ **"Bürgeramt scarcity":** true for 2023–early 2025 (33–34-day averages). By late 2025 it had **eased considerably** (27-day average, same-day slots in Dec 2025). Don't present peak scarcity as current without the date.
- ⚠️ **ImmoScout "373/day":** refers to the **top-10% most-requested listings**, not the average listing. The 2025 analysis gives no Berlin number, only "very tense" and ">100 per listing" for metros.
- ✅ **Ablöse:** the >50% rule is BGH-confirmed. Precision: only the **excess** is void, not the whole deal.
- ✅ **Kaution:** 3× net cold in 3 instalments confirmed. ✅ **Bestellerprinzip:** 2015-06-01 confirmed via §2(1a).
- ✅ **Zweckentfremdung fine up to €500,000:** correct (§7(4) ZwVbG). Typical actual fines: €1,500–2,500 per month per flat.
- ⚠️ **"Döner €7–8+":** Berlin's crowd-sourced median is **€7.00** (Sept 2026). €7.80 comes from a small-sample index. €8+ is premium territory, not typical.
- ℹ️ **"Last 3 payslips":** market custom only. No legal "3" exists; the DSK allows proof of current income with redactions, only after you're chosen.
- ℹ️ **Club-Mate crate:** no credible survey exists, only shop prices (€13–20.50 + €4.50 deposit).
- ℹ️ **Rüge (rent-cap complaint):** under the current §556g(2), a complaint within 30 months recovers money back to the lease start. Older guides say "only from the complaint onward", which is outdated.
