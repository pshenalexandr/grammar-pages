## !!! CORRECTIONS TO THE BRIEF (read first)

1. **"59.1% yes" in the DWE referendum is not the official headline figure.** The official result is **57.6% of participants** (1,035,950 yes out of 1,798,308 participants). 59.1% is yes as a share of *valid* votes (1,035,950 / 1,751,648 = 59.14%). The initiative and some media use 59.1%. If we use it, write "59.1% of valid votes". Safer: "57.6% of voters, over a million people". Source: Landeswahlleiter, https://www.wahlen-berlin.de/abstimmungen/ve2021/AFSPRAES/ergebnisse.html (official final result, 2021).
2. **Mietendeckel "caps/reductions from Nov 23 2020" is only half right.** The rent freeze (at the 18 June 2019 level) and the **caps on new lettings** applied from **23 Feb 2020**. Only the **compulsory reduction of existing rents** (§5, rents more than 20% above the cap) came into force on **23 Nov 2020**. Sources: BVerfG PM 28/2021; Berliner Zeitung 2020-11-23 (below).
3. **The BVerfG verdict wording.** The law was declared "**mit dem Grundgesetz unvereinbar und deshalb nichtig**" ("incompatible with the Basic Law and therefore void"). Under German law nullity applies from the outset (ex tunc), so "void" or "null and void from the start" is correct. Do not write "unconstitutional because of rent levels": the ruling is purely about **legislative competence**. The court cited §§ 556–561 BGB, i.e. federal rent-price law as a whole, as exhaustive, not just the Mietpreisbremse. The provisions are Art. 74(1) no. 1 GG ("bürgerliches Recht") in conjunction with Art. 72(1) GG.
4. **GSW sale total is not ~€2.1bn.** €405M purchase price plus **€1.56bn debt** taken over ≈ **€1.97bn**. The GSW group had ~65,700 residential and commercial units (Tagesspiegel: 65,050 flats). Note: Welt wrote "rund 1,7 Mrd. Euro Schulden", but the Senate/Sarrazin press release says 1.56bn. Use 1.56bn.
5. **DWE second referendum: signature collection had NOT started as of Sept 2026.** The draft law was presented on 26 Sept 2025, and the initiative says the final vote will come in 2027 at the earliest. In Sept 2026 the plan was still "in Vorbereitung".
6. **The Vergesellschaftungsrahmengesetz was passed on 12 March 2026**, but it only takes effect 24 months after promulgation (≈ 28 March 2028) and socialises nothing by itself.
7. **Public-stock history numbers conflict.** Holm/BMGEV give **482,000 (1990) → 273,000 (end 2005)**. ARCH+ says "fast 590.000" in 1990 → 270,000 in 2010. Use 482,000 → 273,000, which is the more specific and widely cited pair, and footnote the other.
8. **Vonovia's 1% pledge** was "insgesamt" (portfolio average, regular §558 increases only, not per tenant and not modernisation or new lettings). Sources disagree slightly on the end date: "bis 2026" (Rosa-Lux) vs "2025–2027" (Jacobin). The original wording is "three years ≤1%, then two years inflation", announced 25 May 2021.
9. **Furnished-listing share depends heavily on the source.** It is 48% (IBB/Senate, 2025), ~25% (vdpResearch), or 21.8% (DMB Mietenmonitor). See §6.

### Verdict (quote precisely)
- DE: "Das Gesetz zur Mietenbegrenzung im Wohnungswesen in Berlin (MietenWoG Bln) … ist mit Artikel 74 Absatz 1 Nummer 1 in Verbindung mit Artikel 72 Absatz 1 des Grundgesetzes **unvereinbar und nichtig**."
- EN (court's own translation): "the Berlin Rent Cap Act is **incompatible with the Basic Law and thus void**."
- Reasoning: rent levels for privately financed housing fall under concurrent legislation ("bürgerliches Recht"). States may legislate only where the Bund has not done so exhaustively. "Da der Bundesgesetzgeber das Mietpreisrecht in den §§ 556 bis 561 BGB abschließend geregelt hat, ist aufgrund der Sperrwirkung des Bundesrechts … kein Raum." The court named the Mietpreisbremse (2015) and its later amendments as evidence of an exhaustive federal balance. Source: PM 28/2021 (2021-04-15).
- Who brought it: **284 members of the Bundestag from CDU/CSU and FDP** (abstract review), plus referrals by **LG Berlin** (12 Mar 2020) and **AG Mitte** (18 May 2020). Source: PM 28/2021; decision text.
- Process quirk: it was decided by **Beschluss without oral hearing**, which surprised observers. Source: n-tv, https://www.n-tv.de/ratgeber/Das-sollten-Mieter-jetzt-beachten-article22495716.html (2021-04-16).
- Effect: void **from the start**. Source: Berliner MieterGemeinschaft, "Nichtigkeitserklärung … als von Anfang an unwirksam zu betrachten", https://www.bmgev.de/mietrecht/mietendeckel-nichtig (n.d., 2021).

### Consequences
- **~1.5 million flats** had their rents frozen. Sources: ZEIT/dpa 2020-11-23 (above); n-tv 2021-04-16 ("betrifft rund 1,5 Millionen Wohnungen").
- **Back-payments:** tenants owed the difference automatically from 15 Apr 2021. **Vonovia, Heimstaden and all six state companies** (Degewo, Gesobau, Gewobag, Howoge, Stadt und Land, WBM) waived claims. Landlord associations BBU/BFW (~1M flats) said they would not use the arrears to terminate leases. Source: Berliner Mieterverein FAQ, https://www.berliner-mieterverein.de/mietendeckel/fragen-und-antworten-zur-mietrechtlichen-rueckabwicklung-des-mietendeckels.htm (2021-04-23).
- Many landlords had written **"Schattenmieten"** (shadow rents) into contracts: the higher rent that would apply if Karlsruhe killed the law. Source: n-tv 2021-04-16.
- The Senate adopted a hardship fund ("Sicher-Wohnen-Hilfe", interest-free loans) on 20 Apr 2021. It closed **30 Jun 2021**. Sources: BMGEV (above); BMV FAQ (above).

### Evidence on effects
- **DIW** (Kholodilin et al., DIW Wochenbericht 8/2021): advertised rents for regulated flats fell **7–11%** relative to unregulated ones. Rental listings roughly **halved** (from >600 to ~300 per week). Potsdam rents rose **~12%**. Only about a **quarter** of post-cap ads quoted a legally compliant rent. https://www.diw.de/documents/publikationen/73/diw_01.c.811443.de/21-8-3.pdf (Feb 2021)
- **Hahn, Kholodilin, Waltl, Fongoni, "Forward to the Past", *Management Science*** (2023-05-22): rents fell 7–11%. A rent gap opened at the Berlin/Brandenburg border. More rentals were converted to owner-occupied units, and part of the supply was "permanently lost". "Overall, negative consequences for renters appear to outweigh positive ones." https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.2023.4775
- **IW Köln** (IW-Trends 3/2021): asking rents **−10.3%**, number of listings **−51.8%**. ImmoScout24 contacts per listing **+67%**. The share of private landlords re-letting via friends and acquaintances rose from 43% to 53%. https://www.iwkoeln.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Studien/IW-Trends/PDF/2021/IW-Trends-2021-03-03_Sagner-Voigtl%C3%A4nder.pdf (2021)
- **ifo** (Dolls, Fuest, Neumeier, Stöhlker, ifo Schnelldienst 74(03), Mar 2021): the market split in two. The regulated segment lagged, while the unregulated segment rose faster than in other big cities. https://ideas.repec.org/a/ces/ifosdt/v74y2021i03p26-32.html

### Expert commission
- The Senate set it up in March/April 2022. **13 members**, **chaired by Prof. Herta Däubler-Gmelin** (former federal justice minister, SPD). The report was **handed over 28 June 2023** in the Rotes Rathaus to Wegner and Gaebler. Sources: Abschlussbericht, https://www.berlin.de/kommission-vergesellschaftung/_assets/abschlussbericht_vergesellschaftung-grosser-wohnungsunternehmen-230627.pdf (2023-06-27); Welt https://www.welt.de/politik/deutschland/article246083870/ (2023-06-28).
- Findings:
  - **Berlin has the legislative competence.** The Bund has not used its competence over socialisation (Art. 74(1) no. 15 GG).
  - Socialisation satisfies Art. 15 GG (unanimous).
  - **9 members:** proportionality is no obstacle. **3 members** dissented in a Sondervotum, and 1 member partly agreed with them.
  - The majority held that compensation **may be below market value (Verkehrswert)**.
  - Source: Abschlussbericht; Freitag https://www.freitag.de/autoren/sabine-nuss/abschlussbericht-vergesellschaftung-von-wohnungskonzernen-moeglich (2023-06-28).

### Politics after
- **CDU/SPD coalition (April 2023)** promised a general "Vergesellschaftungsrahmengesetz" covering housing, water and energy, to take effect only 2 years after promulgation. Source: entwicklungsstadt 2026-09-12.
- The bill was introduced in Dec 2025 and **passed on 12 Mar 2026** with the coalition's votes. It requires a public-interest goal, proportionality and "angemessene Entschädigung". It excludes Art. 14 expropriation and **socialises nothing by itself**. It applies from **28 Mar 2028**. Sources: Haufe https://www.haufe.de/immobilien/wirtschaft-politik/berlin-debatte-um-enteignungen-geht-weiter_84342_487722.html (updated 2026); entwicklungsstadt 2026-09-12.
- The coalition said it would get BVerfG review in the meantime. As of **early Sept 2026, no review proceeding had been filed**: the Senate said in reply to a Linke written question that it had not filed a Normenkontrolle and knew of no one else who had. Source: entwicklungsstadt 2026-09-12.
- **DWE's own law:** presented on **26 Sept 2025**. It covers ~**200,000–220,000 flats**. Each company may keep 3,000 flats ("Selbstbehalt"). The flats would go to a new public body (AöR) "Gemeingut Wohnen". It is intended for a binding Gesetzesvolksentscheid, with the final vote "not before 2027". As of Sept 2026 the plan was still in preparation. The first stage needs 20,000 signatures within 6 months, the second stage runs 4 months. Sources: DWE PM https://dwenteignen.de/aktuelles/neuigkeiten/vergesellschaftungsgesetz-vorgestellt (2025-09-26); Haufe (above); Deutsche Wohnen position page https://www.deutsche-wohnen.com/fakten-positionen/vergesellschaftung-aktueller-stand (n.d., autumn 2026; interested party).
- **Federal counter-move:** the CDU/CSU–SPD **Koalitionsausschuss, night of 1–2 July 2026**, decided: "Um den privaten Wohnungsbau nicht zu gefährden, wird durch Bundesgesetz geregelt, dass die Verstaatlichung privater Mietwohnungsbestände durch Vergesellschaftungsgesetze auf Landesebene nicht mehr möglich ist." Klingbeil: "Wir wollen bauen, nicht enteignen." **No such law had been passed** as of Sept 2026. Sources: Tagesspiegel https://www.tagesspiegel.de/politik/wir-wollen-bauen-nicht-enteignen-bund-untersagt-landern-verstaatlichungen-von-wohnraum-15784792.html (2026-07-02); ZEIT/dpa https://www.zeit.de/news/2026-07/02/koalition-will-verstaatlichung-von-mietwohnungen-verbieten (2026-07-02); Bundestag Drs. 21/7572 https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/21/075/2107572.pdf (2026); entwicklungsstadt 2026-09-12.
- **Five-year score:** "Fünf Jahre später ist auf dieser Grundlage noch keine Wohnung vergesellschaftet worden." Source: entwicklungsstadt 2026-09-12.

### The 2021 sale to the state
- Announced **17 Sept 2021**: Howoge (~8,300), Berlinovo (>4,000) and Degewo (~2,400) bought **~14,750 flats + ~450 commercial units for ~€2.46bn**, debt-financed by the companies with no direct burden on the state budget.
- The original offer was 20,000 flats. About 10,000 of the flats came from DW and 4,250 from Vonovia. State stock rose to ~355,000.
- **All the 1970s/80s buildings contain asbestos** (DW CEO Zahn). Degewo budgeted €84M and Berlinovo €167M extra over 15 years for refurbishment.
- Sources: Senate press release https://www.berlin.de/sen/finanzen/presse/pressemitteilungen/pressemitteilung.1127587.php (2021-09-17); ZEIT/dpa https://www.zeit.de/news/2021-09/16/senat-und-unternehmen-informieren-ueber-wohnungsgeschaeft (2021-09-17); Tagesspiegel https://www.tagesspiegel.de/berlin/diese-wohnungen-erhalt-berlin-von-vonovia-und-deutsche-wohnen-5121376.html (2021-09-17).

### "Zukunfts- und Sozialpakt Wohnen" (25 May 2021)
- "regular rent increases across their Berlin portfolio **as a whole** limited to max **1% p.a. for the next three years**, then **inflation compensation for the two following years**". The modernisation levy was capped at **€2/m²**. The pact also included the offer of ~20,000 flats to the state and 13,000 new builds (one third subsidised).
- Critics: the 1% is a **portfolio average** that covers only regular §558 increases. Vonovia's actual 2020 increases were already only ~0.6–1%.
- Sources: DW release 2021-05-25 (above); Rosa-Luxemburg-Stiftung https://www.rosalux.de/news/id/44404/die-fusion-der-taeuscher (2021-06-02); Jacobin https://jacobin.de/vonovia-deutsche-wohnen-fusion-berlin-wohnungskrise/ (2021-06-03).

### Controversies and verdicts
- **GDPR fine saga (Deutsche Wohnen):**
  - **30 Oct 2019:** the Berlin data-protection commissioner fines DW **€14.5M** for an archive system that could not delete old tenants' data (pay slips, ID copies, bank statements).
  - **18 Feb 2021:** LG Berlin **discontinues** the case: it held that no responsible natural person had been identified.
  - **5 Dec 2023:** **CJEU C-807/21** rules that companies can be fined directly, but only for intentional or negligent breaches.
  - KG Berlin then sent the case back to LG.
  - **9 Jun 2026:** **LG Berlin I fines DW €900,000** for an intentional breach (25 May 2018 – 5 Mar 2019). **The fine is 94% lower than the original**, and the ruling is **not yet final** (Rechtsbeschwerde possible).
  - Sources: LG press release PM 24/2026, https://www.berlin.de/gerichte/presse/pressemitteilungen-der-ordentlichen-gerichtsbarkeit/2026/pressemitteilung.1679703.php (2026-06-09); Taylor Wessing https://www.taylorwessing.com/de/insights-and-events/insights/2023/12/eugh-zu-deutsche-wohnen (2023-12-05); ZEIT/dpa https://www.zeit.de/news/2024-02/22/streit-um-millionen-bussgeld-gegen-deutsche-wohnen (2024-02-22).
- **Heating bills (Vonovia):**
  - 2022 heating bills of up to €6,000 caused an uproar in Dec 2023, e.g. at Mariendorf-Ost and Tempelhofer Damm.
  - **AG Kreuzberg (March 2025)**: a tenant does not have to pay a €4,165.37 demand. The heat meter was only installed in Aug 2022, and estimating consumption was not allowed. Vonovia appealed, so the ruling is not final.
  - The heat contractor G+D is a joint venture of Getec and Deutsche Wohnen.
  - Sources: taz https://taz.de/Fragwuerdige-Nebenkostenabrechnungen/!6074831/ (2025-03-25); Berliner Mieterverein PM https://www.berliner-mieterverein.de/presse/pressearchiv/berliner-mieterverein-kritisiert-vonovia-fuer-heizkosten-mahnungen-und-raet-den-betroffenen-hohe-nachzahlungen-nicht-voreilig-unter-vorbehalt-zu-zahlen-pm2401.htm (2024-01-18); BMV magazine https://www.berliner-mieterverein.de/magazin/online/mm1224/die-heizkostenmaschine.htm (2024-12-01).
- **Kicked out of the Wohnungsbündnis (11 Apr 2025):**
  - Vonovia had justified rent increases with **invented Mietspiegel features** ("gute ÖPNV-Anbindung", "gute Nahversorgung"). Local courts rejected this at least 11 times.
  - It had also raised rents by the legal max of 15% in 3 years instead of the agreed 11%.
  - The Senate turned the alliance into an associations-only body. Vonovia had been the last individual company left: Adler quit in 2023.
  - Sources: Tagesspiegel https://www.tagesspiegel.de/berlin/berliner-wirtschaft/wegen-falsch-begrundeter-mieterhohungen-berliner-senat-wirft-vonovia-aus-dem-wohnungsbundnis-13528984.html (2025-04-11); taz https://taz.de/Vonovia-fliegt-aus-Wohnungsbuendnis/!6078988/ (2025-04-14); nd (2025-04-21).
- **Akelius → Heimstaden (2021):**
  - Heimstaden bought **14,050 Berlin flats** from Akelius as a **share deal**. In a share deal no plot of land is sold, so **no municipal pre-emption right (Vorkaufsrecht) arises**.
  - The kolportiert (reported) price for the whole package was ~€4.5bn.
  - Sources: Tagesspiegel Pankow https://leute.tagesspiegel.de/pankow/macher/2021/11/04/196102/heimstaden-akelius-deal-bezirk-kann-vorkaufsrecht-nicht-ausueben (2021-11-04); MieterEcho https://www.bmgev.de/mieterecho/artikel/heimstaden-wird-big-player-in-berlin (2021).
- **BVerwG 4 C 1/20 (9 Nov 2021):** districts may not exercise the pre-emption right in Milieuschutz areas just because they expect a buyer to displace tenants later. This gutted Berlin's favourite tool. Source: https://www.bverwg.de/pm/2021/70 (2021-11-09).

### The sales
- **GEHAG, 1998:**
  - 74.98% was sold to RSE Grundbesitz (85%) and Hamburgische Landesbank (15%). The state's share was ~50.1%, and Berlin kept 25% + 1 share.
  - Price: **950M DM** for 75%, of which ~650M DM went to the state budget.
  - GEHAG had ~33,500 flats (taz) / 31,299 units (RSE), ~29,000 of them in Berlin. The deal closed **4 Dec 1998**.
  - Sources: taz https://taz.de/33500-Wohnungen-privatisiert/!1316564/ (1998-11-11); RSE ad hoc https://www.ots.at/presseaussendung/OTS_19981204_OTS0211/ (1998-12-04).
- **GSW, 2004:**
  - The red-red Senate (Wowereit/finance senator Sarrazin) approved the sale on **25 May 2004**, notarised **27 May 2004**.
  - Buyers: Cerberus + Whitehall (Goldman Sachs) with 90%, Contest Beteiligungs GmbH with 10%.
  - Price **€405M** incl. a €4M cost lump sum. The buyers took over **€1.56bn debt**.
  - GSW had ~**65,700** residential + commercial units (65,050 flats).
  - The budget had planned only €250M, so the sale brought in €155M "extra". Sarrazin called it a "beachtlicher Erfolg".
  - Sources: BBU / Senate PM https://bbu.de/beitraege/senat-stimmt-verkauf-der-gsw-zu (2004-05-26); Tagesspiegel https://www.tagesspiegel.de/berlin/neue-eigentumer-der-gsw-wollen-freundlich-zu-den-mietern-sein-senat-beschliesst-privatisierung-des-wohnungsunternehmens-1122898.html (2004-05-26); Welt (2004-05-26); Drucksache 15/2968 https://pardok.parlament-berlin.de/starweb/adis/citat/VT/15/DruckSachen/d15-2968.pdf
- **Where they ended up:**
  - GEHAG went to **Deutsche Wohnen** in July 2007, at an enterprise value of €1.84bn for ~27,000 units (incl. care homes and cable business). Source: GSC Research https://gsc-research.de/berichte/61804 (2007-07-03); DW Q3 2007 report.
  - GSW went to **Deutsche Wohnen** in Nov 2013 (91.05% tendered, closed 27 Nov 2013). Source: GSW/DW domination agreement report, SEC exhibit https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1618267/000119312514332159/d781671dex99d.htm (2014).
  - Deutsche Wohnen in turn went to **Vonovia** in 2021.

### Buy-backs
- **Karl-Marx-Allee, 2019:**
  - Deutsche Wohnen wanted to buy blocks there from Predac.
  - The Senate invented the "**gestreckter Erwerb**" ("stretched purchase"): tenants exercised their individual pre-emption rights under §577 BGB and immediately passed the purchase on to Gewobag. 316 households took part by Jan 2019.
  - On **15 Jul 2019** the Senate announced it was buying ~**670 flats** (price undisclosed). In total ~800 flats were re-municipalised.
  - Sources: Tagesspiegel https://www.tagesspiegel.de/berlin/gewobag-ubernimmt-wohnungen-in-der-karl-marx-allee-5311165.html (2019-01-04); SZ https://www.sueddeutsche.de/politik/berlin-immobilien-karl-marx-allee-1.4526079 (2019-07-15); Baustelle Gemeinwohl https://www.baustelle-gemeinwohl.de/baustellen/gestreckter-erwerb-an-der-karl-marx-allee/ (n.d.).
- **ADO → Gewobag, 26 Sept 2019:**
  - **~5,800 flats + 70 commercial units** in Spandau and Reinickendorf.
  - **€920M** (share deal; price minus ~€340M net debt).
  - It was called "the largest re-municipalisation in Berlin's history" at the time.
  - Sources: ADO/Adler release https://www.adler-group.com/investor-relations/ (2019-09-26); Spiegel https://www.spiegel.de/wirtschaft/soziales/berlin-kauft-knapp-6000-wohnungen-von-immobilienkonzern-zurueck-a-1288881.html (2019-09-26); LTO (2019-09-27).
- **Vonovia/DW → Howoge/Degewo/Berlinovo, 17 Sept 2021:** ~14,750 flats for €2.46bn (see §3).

### Price per flat: sold vs bought back (computed; nominal euros, not inflation-adjusted; different portfolios)
| Deal | Calculation | Per flat |
|---|---|---|

**Headline (true with caveats):** Berlin sold GSW for about **€30,000 per flat including debt** (about €6,200 if you count only the cash price). In 2021 it bought back flats from the same corporate family at about **€167,000 each**, roughly **5.6× the 2004 enterprise value per flat**. The flats are not identical, and the figures are not inflation-adjusted.

## 5. TEMPELHOFER FELD

- **Referendum 25 May 2014** ("100% Tempelhofer Feld", held alongside the European election):
  - Yes **739,124 = 64.3% of participants** (29.7% of the electorate); no 343,982 = 29.9%; invalid 66,039 = 5.7%.
  - Turnout **46.1%** (1,149,145 of 2,491,365). **Adopted.**
  - Result: the ThF-Gesetz bans building on the field.
  - Source: Wahlbuch comparison table https://www.berlin.de/wahlen/abstimmungen/deutsche-wohnen-und-co-enteignen/ergebnisse/wahlbuch_ve_endgueltig.pdf (2021).
- **Size:** the whole area is **355 ha**: ~300 ha of field with runways and taxiway + ~55 ha of sealed apron. The airport building is 1.23 km long, was once the "second-largest building in the world" and is "Europe's largest listed monument". The neighbourhoods around it have >700 residents per ha. Source: Senate UVK, https://www.berlin.de/sen/uvk/natur-und-gruen/stadtgruen/stadtgruen-projekte/tempelhofer-feld/ (n.d.).
- **Bigger than Monaco?** Yes. Monaco is ~208 ha (≈2.08 km²), and even the 300-ha field alone is bigger. Sources: Tagesspiegel "eine Fläche größer als Monaco" https://www.tagesspiegel.de/berlin/das-tempelhofer-feld-der-traume-interessante-ideen-aus-denen-nichts-wurde-9555914.html (2023-03-26); Monaco area per Leander Wattig blog (2021-01-14). Verify the Monaco figure against an official Monaco source before printing.
- **2023–2026 debate:**
  - The CDU/SPD coalition agreement called for a "Neubewertung" by Berliners.
  - The Senate ran a dialogue process with **275** randomly selected residents invited. Its ideas competition launched on **13 Nov 2024**, received **164 entries**, and picked **6 equal-ranked winners**; they were exhibited until 5 Oct 2025. Source: https://thf-dialog.berlin.de/ (n.d.).
  - **20 May 2026:** architects Hans Kollhoff and Tobias Nöfer ("Zuhause am Tempelhofer Feld") propose **21,400 flats** on the outer edge in Gründerzeit style.
  - **22 May 2026:** Wegner says "Das Land Berlin könnte dieses Projekt selbst entwickeln".
  - INSA poll for the IHK (7–14 Apr 2026, n=1,000): **59% for** "moderate edge development", 25% against. Sources: Tagesspiegel https://www.tagesspiegel.de/berlin/architektenplan-fur-21400-wohnungen-wegner-pruft-randbebauung-des-tempelhofer-feldes-durchs-land-berlin-15631939.html (2026-05-22); Morgenpost (2026-05-20).
  - CDU parliamentary leader Stettner called edge development a **condition** for any coalition: "Die beste Volksbefragung ist die Wahl am 20. September." Source: ZEIT/dpa https://www.zeit.de/news/2026-05/25/streit-um-bebauung-des-tempelhofer-feldes-neu-entfacht (2026-05-25).
  - **Linke and Grüne oppose building, and the SPD is split.** The AfD would want a new referendum first. Source: ZEIT/dpa (2026-07-06).
  - **Implication now:** the CDU lost (18.8%) and the Linke-led R2G talks started on 8 Oct 2026, so edge development looks unlikely unless CDU–Grüne–SPD forms instead.
  - The citizens' Feldforum (12 Sept 2026) demands that the land-use plan (FNP) be rezoned entirely as green space. Source: https://tempelhofer-feld.berlin.de/documents/936/20260922_Fefo_Empfehlungen-zusammengef%C3%BCgt-1.pdf (2026-09-22).

## 6. CHARACTERS (flip-card facts)

**The Bestandsmieter (sitting tenant)**
- The average Mietspiegel 2024 rent is **€7.21/m²** net cold, up just 5 cents (+0.7%) on 2023. Source: BBU https://bbu.de/beitraege/berliner-mietspiegel-2024-veroeffentlicht (2024-05-30). Senate PM https://www.berlin.de/sen/sbw/presse/pressemeldungen/pressemitteilung.1451496.php gives ~1.4M flats covered by the Mietspiegel.
- State-company sitting tenants pay on average €7.09/m². Source: entwicklungsstadt 2026-04-16.
- Funny-true: under the Mietendeckel their rent was legally frozen at its level on **18 June 2019**, a Tuesday picked because that was the day the Senate adopted the cornerstones.

**The Newcomer**
- The median asking rent for unfurnished listings in 2025 was **€15.78/m²**, up from €7.20 in 2012, i.e. **~2.2× the Mietspiegel average**. There were only **40,014** regular listings in 2025, vs 64,483 in 2012. Source: IBB Wohnungsmarktbericht 2025 tables, https://www.ibb.de/media/dokumente/publikationen/berliner-wohnungsmarkt/wohnungsmarktbericht/2025/ibb-wohnungsmarktbericht-angebotsmieten_2012-2025.pdf ; Senate answer S19/26285 https://pardok.parlament-berlin.de/starweb/adis/citat/VT/19/SchrAnfr/S19-26285.pdf (2026).
- During the rent cap, contacts per ImmoScout listing rose 67%. Source: IW 2021.
- **DMB Mietenmonitor:** >45% of listed older flats likely breach the Mietpreisbremse, and almost a third exceed the "Mietpreisüberhöhung/Mietwucher" thresholds. Source: https://mieterbund.de/app/uploads/2025/12/FactSheet_DMB_Mietenmonitor_2025.pdf (Dec 2025).

**The Big Landlord (Vonovia/Deutsche Wohnen)**
- 138,354 Berlin units, 0.7% vacancy (FY2025).
- It was expelled from the Senate's housing alliance for citing **"good bus connections"** as a Mietspiegel feature that does not exist (2025-04-11).
- Its €14.5M GDPR fine became €900,000 after 6½ years (2026-06-09).

**The Senat**
- The responsible department is the Senatsverwaltung für Stadtentwicklung, Bauen und Wohnen. The **Bausenator is Christian Gaebler (SPD)**, in office since 2023 and still acting while the new government forms. Sources: BBU 2026-06 Roadmap 2.0 PM (Gaebler quoted as Senator); Welt 2023-06-28.
- The governing mayor is Kai Wegner (CDU), acting.
- Funny-true: one Senate sold 65,700 GSW units for €405M in 2004. Later Senates paid €2.46bn for 14,750 flats bought back from the company that had swallowed GSW.

**The Berliner Mieterverein**
- Founded **1888** as "Verein Berliner Wohnungsmiether"; it has had its current name since 1934. It now has **"über 190.000"** members (website), and grew by ~7,000 in 2024. Sources: https://www.berliner-mieterverein.de/beitreten.htm (n.d., 2025/26); BMV magazine https://www.berliner-mieterverein.de/magazin/online/mm0625/delegiertenversammlung.htm (2025-05-27).
- Funny-true: dues in 1888 were **40 Pfennig a quarter**. In **1963** it had only **2,300 members**, because rent control made it look unnecessary. Source: BMV history https://www.berliner-mieterverein.de/magazin/online/mm0919/wie-sich-der-berliner-mieterverein-seit-1888-entwickelte-vom-fragekasten-zur-schlagkraeftigen-interessenvertretung-091924.htm (2019-09-04).

**Karlsruhe (BVerfG)**
- It killed the Mietendeckel in a written **Beschluss** without an oral hearing, decided 25 Mar 2021 and published 15 Apr 2021. The case was brought by **284 Bundestag MPs** (CDU/CSU and FDP).
- The ruling never said a rent freeze is wrong as such. It said only that **Berlin was the wrong level of government** to impose one.
- Ironic coda: the CDU/SPD Senate's Rahmengesetz waits two years "for Karlsruhe", yet nobody had filed a case as of Sept 2026.

**The Möbliert-auf-Zeit listing**
- Share of all Berlin rental listings (IBB/RegioKontext via the Senate): 13% (2012) → **57% (2023 peak)** → **48% (2025)**. In 2025 there were 36,959 such listings vs 40,014 regular ones.
- The average all-in asking rent is **€24.14/m²** (2025). Source: Senate answer S19/26285 (2026).
- Other counts, using different methods: vdpResearch ~25% peak (2025-06-02, https://www.vdpresearch.de/vom-nischenmarkt-zum-trendthema/); DMB Mietenmonitor 21.8% (Dec 2025).
- The Senate itself cautions that a furnished flat reappears **several times a year**, so listing shares overstate actual flats. Its rough estimate is that about a third of the ~85–90k new leases per year are furnished/temporary.
- Funny-true: a 20 m² "Havens Living" apartment in Charlottenburg was offered at €1,349, i.e. **>€67/m²**. Source: BMV magazine https://www.berliner-mieterverein.de/magazin/online/mm1225/berlin-verliert-massenhaft-bezahlbaren-wohnraum-durch-moeblierte-vermietung-auf-zeit.htm (2025-12-03).

## FUNNY-BUT-TRUE FACTS (with sources)

1. **The landlord Berliners voted to expropriate got bought the next working day.** Berlin voted to socialise "Deutsche Wohnen & Co" on Sunday 26 Sept 2021. On Monday 27 Sept, Vonovia announced it now controlled 50.49% of Deutsche Wohnen. Sources: wahlen-berlin.de; Reuters 2021-09-27.
2. **Berlin's privatised landlords all ended up in one company.** GEHAG (sold 1998) and GSW (sold 2004) both later landed at Deutsche Wohnen (2007, 2013), which landed at Vonovia (2021). Sources: taz 1998-11-11; BBU 2004-05-26; GSC Research 2007-07-03; SEC exhibit 2014; Reuters 2021-09-27.
3. **Buy high, sell low.** GSW went for ~€30k per unit including debt (2004). Seventeen years later the state paid ~€167k per flat to buy flats back from the same corporate family (2021). Sources: BBU 2004-05-26; Senate PM 2021-09-17.
4. **The buy-back came with free asbestos.** At the 2021 handover the Deutsche Wohnen CEO said all the 1970s/80s buildings contained asbestos, and the state firms set aside €84M and €167M for it. Source: Tagesspiegel 2021-09-17.
5. **A rent rise justified by "good bus connections".** Vonovia used Mietspiegel features that do not exist (good public transport, good shops nearby), lost repeatedly in court, and was thrown out of the Senate's housing alliance. Sources: taz 2025-04-14; Tagesspiegel 2025-04-11.
6. **A €14.5M fine shrank to €900k.** The GDPR fine against Deutsche Wohnen took 6½ years, a trip to the EU Court of Justice and a re-trial, and fell by ~94%. Not final yet. Source: LG Berlin I PM 24/2026 (2026-06-09).
7. **An old airfield bigger than a country.** Tempelhofer Feld (355 ha; ~300 ha of field) is bigger than Monaco (~208 ha), and its terminal was once the world's second-largest building. Sources: berlin.de/sen/uvk; Tagesspiegel 2023-03-26.
8. **The rent cap made Potsdam the winner.** As Berlin rents fell under the cap, asking rents in unregulated Potsdam next door rose ~12%. Source: DIW Wochenbericht 8/2021.
9. **A law that is coming in 2028.** The 2026 "Vergesellschaftungsrahmengesetz" passed in March 2026, deliberately waits two years to take effect "so Karlsruhe can check it", and no one has asked Karlsruhe. Source: entwicklungsstadt 2026-09-12.
10. **In 1963 nobody needed a tenants' union.** The Berliner Mieterverein had 2,300 members then; today it has over 190,000. Source: BMV 2019-09-04; BMV website.
11. **Half the "flats for rent" aren't really flats for rent.** In 2023, 57% of Berlin rental listings were furnished/temporary. Source: Senate answer S19/26285 (2026).
12. **The rent cap's lone abstainer is still around.** The 2020 vote was 85–64 with exactly one abstention: SPD MP Lars Düsterhöft. In 2026 he won the SPD's only direct mandate in all of Berlin (Treptow-Köpenick 3). Sources: abgeordnetenwatch 2020-01-30; KAS election analysis Sept 2026.
